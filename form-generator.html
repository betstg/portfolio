Form generator

I built this in 2022 to stop arguing about form fields in comments. You describe every field once, with its type, its limits and where its data goes, and the same description produces the form, the schema and the contract engineering builds against.

Add fields on the left. The working form, the spec, the SQLite table and the MongoDB validator all come out on the right, and change as you type.
